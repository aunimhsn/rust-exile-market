Window · layout
rust-exile-market

Breakpoints

  • Narrow, under 500 px: one column. The settings replace the content.
  • Medium, from 500 to 999 px: the item on the left, its prices on the right. The settings open as a side panel.
  • Wide, from 1000 px: bigger item and headline price, plus the price history below.

Proposals to decide

  • Headline currency: above the table of the three currencies, the price in the most readable one: Divine Orbs from 1 Divine Orb, Exalted Orbs below. Switch to None to compare.
  • Item icon: the price database gives an icon for each item. The grey square shows where it would go.
  • Unidentified unique: the list of the possible uniques shows the price of each one.
  • Data age and source: in a status bar at the bottom, with the name of the price database.
  • French messages: formal ("Copiez"), like the French game client.
