Window · layout
rust-exile-market

Breakpoints

  • Narrow, under 500 px: one column; the item stats are folded, and the offers show only the price and the listing time. The settings replace the content.
  • Medium, from 500 to 999 px: the item and its stats on the left, the price and the offers on the right. The settings open as a side panel.
  • Wide, from 1000 px: three columns: the item and its stats, the price and its 7-day history, the offers with the rolls of each one.

Sources

  • Price, offer count and history: poe2scout, refreshed every hour.
  • Offers: the trade site, one search when an item is copied. Prices far below the poe2scout price (under a quarter of it) are struck through and marked with a question mark: they are usually bait or mistakes.
  • Item stats: the copied text. The roll bars need the advanced copy (Ctrl+Alt+C), which gives the range of each stat.

Proposals to decide

  • Headline currency: above the table of the three currencies, the price in the most readable one: Divine Orbs from 1 Divine Orb, Exalted Orbs below. Switch to None to compare.
  • Icons: the orbs come from assets/currencies/, bundled in the executable; the item icons are loaded from the Grinding Gear Games CDN, at the address given by the price database. Rare and unidentified items have no icon: the first letter of their item class stands in.
  • Unidentified unique: the list of the possible uniques shows the price of each one.
  • Data age and source: in a status bar at the bottom.
  • Disclaimer: at the bottom of the settings.
  • French messages: formal ("Copiez"), like the French game client.
