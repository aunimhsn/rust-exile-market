# Images

The Grinding Gear Games [developer guidelines](https://www.pathofexile.com/developer/docs) forbid using their intellectual property to make money, imitating their official platforms with their logos, and presenting a tool as made or approved by them; every public tool must show the sentence "This product isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Grinding Gear Games in any way." Showing the game icons in a free tool fits these rules, like poe2db, poe.ninja and poe2scout do. The application follows these rules:

- `assets/currencies/` holds the Exalted, Divine and Chaos Orbs of the price block (64 px). They are embedded in the executable, so that they show offline. [assets/NOTICE.md](../assets/NOTICE.md) states that they belong to Grinding Gear Games and are not covered by the project license.
- `assets/exchange/` holds the icons of the 771 items of the currency exchange of the trade site, the currencies an offer may be priced in (fix of the prices in other currencies, 2026-11-01), shrunk to 36 px (twice the 18 points of an offer row) and centered on a transparent square, about 2.7 MB, with their English and French names and their group in `currencies.ndjson`. `tools/exchange-icons` (`cargo run -p exchange-icons`) takes them from `/api/trade2/data/static` and the CDN of the game (`web.poecdn.com`), the images of the trade site itself: run it again at a new league or after a patch that adds items. `build.rs` embeds them, the table of their ids written from the folder; they show offline, and nothing is downloaded while the player plays. A currency added since the last run has no icon: its offers are left out of the list, logged once per page ("priced in an unknown currency"), the sign to run the tool again.
- Only 639 images serve the 771 items: the Greater and Perfect Orbs share the icon of their orb, the Thaumaturgic Fluxes, the Legacies and the uncut gems one icon each. So every currency is written with its name after its icon (see specifications, Offers block).
- The window shows no item icon: no other game image is downloaded or added to the repository, and none is extracted from the game files.
- `assets/logo/` holds the logo and the emblem of the application, original creations of the project, without the Path of Exile logo.
- The disclaimer is shown in the README and in the application settings.
