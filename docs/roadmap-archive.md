# Roadmap archive

The detail of the steps done, moved from the [roadmap](../roadmap.md) once their pull request is merged. The pull requests and the Git history hold the rest.

- [x] **0. Mock-ups**: narrow layout first (mobile first): editable Excalidraw wireframe in `docs/wireframes/`, then the interactive HTML mock-up `docs/mockups/narrow-styles.html` (three styles compared with tabs, style B · Slate chosen and iterated) of every kind of item, state and error, with the item stats, the filters, the offers and their tooltip; the visual style, the logo and the emblem written in specs.md and `assets/logo/`.
- [x] **1. Application window**: egui window with the visual style (tokens, embedded fonts), the header (emblem and name, league block, gear block), the status bar and the empty state (logo, Ctrl + C), texts translated with Fluent in English and French, X11 backend on Linux, no console window on Windows, Linux release target moved to glibc.
- [x] **2. Clipboard watching**: detect the copied Path of Exile 2 item texts and show them raw in the window; the clipboard is read on its own thread, which wakes the window up when an item arrives (eframe only redraws on events, and the game has the focus); `App::logic` takes in the events and `App::ui` only draws; the warning band when the clipboard can't be read; the log file; verify on Bazzite KDE with the game, and on Windows.
- [x] **3. Item parser**: read the item class, rarity, name, base type (without the "Exceptional" prefix), stack size, item level, quality, sockets, requirements, granted skill, states and modifiers, in English and in French, with tests on real copied items (an exceptional base in French: `Exceptionnel` or `Exceptionnelle` before the base; the English `Exceptional` is tested on a written item; the French `supérieur` after the base, from Exiled Exchange 2, is to check on the first superior item copied in French). Nothing that can panic runs on a copied text before it is recognized as an item, so that a panic message never carries a text copied outside the game into the log.
- [x] **4. Interface language**: the language of the system at startup when the interface has it, English otherwise (the Fluent files exist since step 1; the setting to choose it comes in step 5).
- [x] **5. Settings**: settings panel (language: System, English or Français, kept in a type of its own rather than `Language`, the interface switched at once, always on top, disclaimer) opened by the gear, the header returning the clicked block as an action to the application, saved in the configuration directory, window size and position restored.
- [x] **6. Title bar**: the window without the title bar of the system (its icon, its title and a Maximize button the narrow window doesn't need, which X11 can't hide one by one), and one line of 28 px in place of it and of the header: the emblem, the league block tinted with the color of its trade (without the SOFTCORE / HARDCORE label, said by its tooltip), the Settings, Minimize and Close icons without borders; the empty space moves the window, the edges resize it; the version and the author in the status bar while the settings are open; the empty state with the emblem as a faint watermark instead of the full logo; wireframe and mock-up first; checked on KDE (X11) and Windows.
- [x] **7. Price data**, in two pull requests: the leagues (7a), then the prices (7b): poe2scout client, leagues and the league block of the title bar (softcore and hardcore colors, list of the leagues on click, which the title bar already returns as an action; the chosen league saved in `settings.toml`; at the end of a league, the permanent league of its mode without a message, both shown as "Standard", then the bubble of a new league with its Switch pill, closed for good per league; the names of the permanent leagues to check in the league list of poe2scout), several warnings at once in the warning band, download per category (the requests spaced one per second by the poe2scout client itself; the league block, before the leagues arrive, without the "Change the league" tooltip of a block that does nothing yet), cache, hourly refresh, error handling, following the [poe2scout usage](poe2scout-usage.md) rules. In 7b, the prices in Exalted Orbs with the rates of the Chaos and Divine Orbs and the 7-day history, a download interrupted when the player changes the league (the market thread takes orders from the window), a failed download tried again every minute, the warnings of the prices and their age in the status bar (refreshed every minute), the prices of a league without cache shown category by category, and their download in place of the invitation to copy an item (a bar, and a funny sentence drawn at random among ten).
- [x] **8. French item names**: trade site data in both languages, cached for a day, and the French to English dictionary used to find the price of a French item; the base type of a magic item, which the parser leaves inside its whole name (`Crackling Temple Maul of the Brute`), found among the base types of the item data. The price of a fixed-name item found in the prices (an unidentified unique: each unique of its base type) and written to the log until the price block of step 9 shows it; the HTTP agent and the pace of the requests shared by the poe2scout and trade site clients.
- [x] **9. Price display**, in two pull requests: the item block (9a), then the price block (9b): item block (name, Check on trade, tags, summary, requirements) and price block of the fixed-name items (three currencies with their orbs, stack value and unit price, offer count or stack size, trend, uncertainty warning, special cases), status bar; the properties the summary needs (damage, attack speed, critical hit chance) read as typed values for the DPS, and the full name of an exceptional base (`Exceptional Chiming Staff`), which the parser keeps as a flag.
- [ ] **10. Item stats and filters**, in three pull requests:
  - **10a, the stats** (done, #20): Show stats pill (it stays unfolded for the next items until the player folds it); the item block as in the mock-up (base · class, base values with the colors of the attributes, colored requirements, tags, no T1 tag); the stats grouped (Enchant, Runes, Implicit, Prefixes / Suffixes or Explicit, Unique), those that vary first, two lines at most with a tooltip, a hybrid modifier as one block; the roll bars of the uniques (a unique copied with Ctrl+C gets its ranges from the implicit and explicit modifiers of the price database, kept in the price cache); a rare or magic item shows its tier and the range of that tier, its bar over every tier coming with the modifier tables of step 13; the content scrolls under the title bar.
  - **Interlude, token economy** (done; before 10b, asked by the owner on 2026-10-26): split `docs/mockups/narrow-styles.html` into smaller files (one per screen or section, the shared styles and scripts apart), so that a change reads only what it needs; move the generic process rules kept in the memory (wireframes before mock-ups, design changes everywhere, small code reviews, real-app screenshots) into `CLAUDE.md`; then keep to one session per sub-step, a few screenshots, one code review per pull request.
