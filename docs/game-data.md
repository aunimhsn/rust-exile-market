# Game data

The data of the game the application needs beyond the copied text: the id of each modifier line on the trade site (step 13b, to search it), the category of each base (step 13h, for its usual sockets), and the range of every tier of a modifier (step 13j1, for the roll bar and the bounds of the range field). Studied in step 13a, on 2026-10-26, on the copied items of the parser tests.

## Sources compared

| Source | What it gives | Languages | Updated | Rejected or kept |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Trade site, `/api/trade2/data/items` | The base types in each language, grouped by kind (`armour`, `weapon`…), without their category | Every language of the trade site | Always current | Kept for the names of the bases (step 8), not for their category: a group holds the helmets, the body armours and the shields together. |
| Trade site, `/api/trade2/data/stats` | One template per stat and group (`explicit.stat_3299347043`, `# to maximum Life`), one per option for an option stat (`enchant.stat_2954116742\|46224`, `Allocates Arcane Alchemy`), 36 pseudo stats | Every language of the trade site | Always current | **Kept as a second lookup** (13d): a template writes one side of its stat only (`#% reduced Charm Charges used`, without the `increased` one), keeps the `+` sign or not, and ends some with a note the copied text never writes (`(Local)`, `(Global)`, `(Jewel)`, `(Charm)`, `(Flask)`, `(Gold Piles)`, `(Tablets)`). No tier. |
| Exiled Exchange 2, `renderer/public/data/<language>/items.ndjson`, MIT license | Each item by its name in the language: its English name (`refName`), its category (`craftable.category`: `Body Armour`, `Shield`…), its tags | English, French and 8 others | With its stats | **Kept for the category of the bases** (13h). |
| [Exiled Exchange 2](https://github.com/Kvan7/Exiled-Exchange-2), `renderer/public/data/<language>/stats.ndjson`, MIT license | For each stat: its game id (`base_maximum_life`), its English template (`ref`), the strings that match it in the language, with `negate` for the `reduced` variants, its trade ids by group (explicit, implicit, fractured, desecrated, rune, enchant, crafted, pseudo…), and the side that is better (`better`) | English, French and 8 others | At each patch of the game (2026-06, 2026-09) | **Kept for the stat ids.** Generated from the game files by its own tool. No tier. |
| [RePoE fork](https://github.com/repoe-fork/repoe), its data in [`repoe-fork/poe2`](https://github.com/repoe-fork/poe2) (`data/mods.json`, `data/base_items.json`, `data/stat_translations/stat_descriptions.json`, also served at `https://repoe-fork.github.io/poe2/`); MIT license for the code, the data owned by Grinding Gear Games | Every modifier of the game: its group (`type`), prefix or suffix, its required level, its stats with their ranges, and the tags of the bases where it appears (`spawn_weights`); the tags of each base; the English text of each stat | English only | Within days of a patch (last data 2026-10-19) | **Kept for the tiers.** |
| [poe2db](https://poe2db.tw) | The same tiers, as web pages | Many | Fast | Rejected: no API, its pages would have to be scraped. |

## How they fit together

1. **A copied line → its stat** (`src/game_data/lines.rs`, step 13b). The numbers of the line become `#` (with their sign and the ranges of the advanced copy taken out): `Adds 10(1-16) to 273(239-300) Lightning Damage` → `Adds # to # Lightning Damage`. The template is looked up among the strings of Exiled Exchange 2 in the language of the item, which gives the stat, its English template and its trade ids in each group. The group of the line picks the trade ids: explicit, implicit, fractured, desecrated, crafted, rune, and enchant for the enchantments and the modifiers of the corruption.
   - **The sign** goes into the value (`+15% to Fire Resistance` is `#% to Fire Resistance`), or stays before `#` for the strings that write it (`-#% to all Elemental Resistances per Socket filled`).
   - **The `reduced` variants** are strings of the same stat marked `negate`: `20% reduced Charm Charges used` is -20 of `#% increased Charm Charges used`.
   - **A number written in the string** stays written (`retain 40% of Glory`): the strings with the most `#` are looked up first, then those that keep some numbers. A string without `#` gives its value (`Blind Enemies on Hit` is a chance of 100%), and an option stat its option (the skill of `# to Level of all Arc Skills`).
   - **A stat written on several lines** (`Spells fire # additional Projectiles` / `Spells fire Projectiles in a circle`) is looked up before its lines one by one, within one modifier: in the copy without the advanced descriptions, where each line is a modifier of its own, such a line isn't found.
   - **A local and a global stat** with the same text (`# to Accuracy Rating` of a weapon or of a ring) keep both ids: the copied text doesn't tell them apart, and both are searched in a `count` group of minimum 1 (13e2), which finds the item with either of them.
   - The no-break spaces of French are read as spaces.
   - **The templates of the trade site** (`src/game_data/trade_site.rs`, step 13d) are looked up after the strings of Exiled Exchange 2, the same way, for a line they don't know or have no id for in its group (a modifier added by a patch). A template is looked up without the note at its end, and with it in case a line writes it; the templates written the same are one stat with all their ids (a local and a global stat). Once indexed, the templates leave the names kept in memory (they stay in the cache). The value of such a line is written as its template: 20 for `20% reduced Charm Charges used` and `#% reduced Charm Charges used`. In another language than English, its words are those of the English template of its id, for the better side of the tooltip (13c).
   - A line found nowhere, without an id in its group, or found on the trade site only, is written to the log.
2. **A modifier → its tiers** (`src/game_data/modifiers.rs`, step 13j1). The generator writes each line of a modifier of RePoE with an English template of Exiled Exchange 2. Each string of the line (`{0:+d} to [Accuracy|Accuracy] Rating`) is written without its links and with `#` for its values, and the first one that is a string of a stat of Exiled Exchange 2 gives the template of that stat: `Loads # additional bolts` and `Loads an additional bolt` give `Loads an additional bolt`, `Upgrades Radius to Medium` gives `Upgrades Radius to #`. So the template leads to the modifiers whatever their game stats: one template can be several, a global one and a local one (`base_maximum_energy_shield` and `local_energy_shield`). The values of a line are those of the string that writes the most of them (`Loads {0} additional bolts`), or of its stats when no string writes one and its template has one (`Upgrades Radius to Medium` is 1 of `Upgrades Radius to #`; `Instant Recovery` has none); their ranges are written as the game shows them, the handlers of the string applied (per second for a value stored per minute, a hundredth for one stored per ten thousand), with the sign of the stat. A copied line without number takes the value its string of Exiled Exchange 2 gives (`Loads an additional bolt` is 1), the value of the stat: the line keeps what it is divided by to be shown (1000 for milliseconds shown in seconds). The base type of the item gives its tags (`Gemini Crossbow` → `crossbow`, `ranged`, `two_hand_weapon`…), written by the generator in the bases of each language from their English name (`refName` of Exiled Exchange 2). The modifiers of the base for a copied modifier are those, prefix or suffix as the header of the copy says (none for a header the parser doesn't know), whose lines are the templates of the copied lines, and whose first weight with a tag of the base is above zero; grouped by `type` and sorted by required level, the last one is tier 1. A `type` can roll other ranges on other bases: the added lightning damage of a two-handed weapon isn't that of a one-handed one, nor that of a bow.
3. **Several groups for one stat**, e.g. `#% increased Physical Damage` alone or with Accuracy Rating: the modifier with the same lines as the copied one, in any order, and a tier of it: with the advanced copy, the ranges written tell the tier, and the values only choose among tiers of the same ranges (those it holds first, then give or take the rounding of the game), so that a value out of its tier keeps it (a sanctified item, `+74(80-89) to maximum Mana`, step 13k2a); without them, a tier that holds the copied values. Without the advanced copy, a value can be in two tiers (30 pairs of tiers of added damage overlap) or in two modifiers (on a ring, the prefix and the suffix of rarity, `6–10` and `8–11`): the best tier is taken, then the first modifier of the file. The application refuses such a copy since step 13j4a, and took this case out of the code in step 13j4b: a modifier whose header doesn't tell its affix has no tiers. A modifier in no tier has no tiers: a modifier of a lord of the Abyss (Amanamu…) is desecrated with a range of its own, outside the tables, while an ordinary desecrated modifier (`of Siphoning`) is found in them.
4. **A copy of the in-game trade → its affixes and tiers** (`trade_tiers` in `src/game_data/modifiers.rs`, step 13k2e3b). Such a copy writes each explicit line alone, without affix, tier nor range (step 13k2e2). Each line, and each run of up to three consecutive lines of the same origin (the lines of the hybrids follow each other on the owner's copies), is looked up among the prefixes and among the suffixes of the base, by its values only; a value in several tiers takes the worst of them among the tiers the item level allows. Then the readings of the item are looked at (at most 4096, the lines alone first): the best has one fractured modifier at most (a Fracturing Orb fractures one modifier of an item not fractured yet), then fits the prefixes and suffixes the item can have, the modifiers whose header tells their affix (an unrevealed one) taking their room, then reads the most lines with a tier (`30% increased Physical Damage` of a weapon is only held by the hybrid with the accuracy, the pure modifier starting at 40%; second code review of 13k2e3b), then makes the most modifiers: the lines are read alone unless they must be a hybrid, the first reading on a tie. A line without tier for another reason (Amanamu, a line copied before a patch) may be taken into a hybrid with the line next to it when its values fall in it: far rarer, and none on the checked items. Two pure modifiers of low tiers often fit a hybrid too (`30% increased Energy Shield` T6 and `+30 to maximum Life` T8 of a helmet, the hybrid T3 `27–32 · 26–32`; code review of 13k2e3b, owner's choice on 2026-10-29). A line that can be a prefix or a suffix keeps the affixes that still fit, the other one left out without a word, the room making it certain (owner's choice in step 13k2e3c2): on the owner's tiara, the fractured `41% increased Energy Shield` and `+34 to maximum Mana` are one hybrid (read apart, the tiara would have four prefixes and two fractured modifiers), and its `18% increased Rarity of Items found` is a suffix since its three prefixes are full. A line no other reading holds is split into two modifiers of two types with its stat, which the game writes as one line (step 13k2e4a): a pure modifier of the line, and another, pure or a hybrid whose other lines follow or precede it with the same origin (the `258% increased Physical Damage` of the owner's bow, the pure T1 `170–179` at 179 and the hybrid T1 `75–79 · 175–200` with the `+195 to Accuracy Rating` after it, at 79: the only way that fits its 3 prefixes); each pair of tiers the item level allows whose ranges, added up, hold the value is a way, the ways that hold it within the ranges first, then those that hold it give or take the rounding. The split counts two affixes in the room, so that a split that overflows it loses (with 2 prefixes, the `258%` stays without tier and the accuracy a pure prefix of tier 5), and it comes after the lines read with a tier and before the modifiers in the order of the readings: a line one modifier holds isn't split (`18% increased Rarity of Items found` would also fit two of 9%), nor a fractured line, which would be two fractured modifiers. When two readings as good split a line in other ways, or one of them only splits it, the line stays without tier and the others are read again: `33% increased Rarity of Items found` of a helmet is a prefix and a suffix of T1 and T2, or of T2 and T1, while `37%` is both of T1 (19 and 18). None of the 11 advanced copies of the test below is split. A line no tier holds is read without tier and isn't counted: a stat the tables don't know, a modifier of a lord of the Abyss, or a line split in more than one way. When no reading fits, every affix is kept, and the log says so. A hybrid whose other line is read on its own (a sanctified value out of its tier) counts twice and may rule out an affix wrongly: a rare case, left as it is. Checked on the 11 rare and magic items of the advanced copy written as the in-game trade writes them (a test): no hybrid made up, 43 of the 54 modifiers find the affix and the tier the advanced copy writes, 9 find no tier (the 2 modifiers of an essence, a line copied before a patch, 4 of Amanamu, 2 sanctified values out of their tier), and the rarity of the runeforged crown stays a prefix or a suffix, the line of Amanamu after it unknown. Then the modifiers are read again as the advanced copy writes them (`with_trade_tiers`, step 13k2e3c1): the lines of a hybrid one modifier, a prefix or a suffix, its tier, and on each value its stat counts the range of that tier, so that the roll bars, the range fields and the tiers are shown as for the advanced copy; a line that can be a prefix or a suffix stays explicit, without affix nor tier, with the tier and ranges of each affix (`TierDoubt::EitherAffix`, step 13k2e3c2), and a line without tier stays as it was. The session does it from the copy as the parser read it, once the base is known: at once for a rare item, once the names of the trade site tell the base of a magic item; when that joins the lines of a hybrid, or splits them again (names that no longer know the base), the choices of the player on the lines are forgotten, the filters being kept by the place of their line (code review of 13k2e3c1). On the same 11 items, the 43 modifiers found have the affix, the tier, the origin and the ranges the advanced copy writes (a test).
5. **A line → its rolls over every tier** (`src/tier_rolls.rs`, step 13j2), once per copied item, with the advanced copy only: the ranges of the tiers follow the values of each line of the modifier, so each line, a line of a hybrid too, gets for each number its stat counts the range from the lowest roll of its worst tier to the highest of tier 1, without its sign as the game writes it, and for a line of one number the tiers of these two rolls (`+86 to maximum Life` of a body armour: 10 of T13 to 214 of T1). Its bar, its range field and the tooltip of the field read them. The base type is the one its search looks for: a magic item gets its rolls once the names of the trade site tell its base.

## Checks on the copied items

- **Stat ids:** every modifier line of the English and French items found its stat, except the lines of the "fixed value" marker of the game, which the parser already takes out.
- **Tiers:** the tier written by the advanced copy and its range, 23 lines of 5 rare and magic items (a crossbow, a bow, a mace, a staff and a body armour in French): `Electrocuting` T2 `1–16 to 239–300` among 10 tiers, `Champion's` T4 `45–54 · 98–123` (the hybrid with Accuracy), the energy shield T4 `61–73` among 11, the spirit T5 `43–46` among 8, the life regeneration T3 `1386–1740` per minute shown as `23.1–29` per second. The values of some stats are stored scaled: per minute, per ten thousand (`7–7.9%` leech is `700–790`); the advanced copy gives the scale to check them against. With the tables of step 13j1 (export 0.5.5.4.2 of RePoE), 21 lines find their tier and range again (a test); two were copied before a patch: the leech of the crossbow, written T1, is T2 since a tier `8–8.9` was added above it, and the lightning damage `1–4 to 46–66` of the mace no longer exists; the two lines of Amanamu are outside the tables.
- **The tables against the trade site,** on 2026-10-27 (and again on the tables as merged, 2026-10-28): the offers fetched give the name, the tier (`P7`, `S1`), the required level and the ranges of each modifier (`explicitMods[].mods`). 374 prefixes and suffixes of the cheapest rare and magic offers of 15 categories (body armours, helmets, gloves, boots, shields, rings, amulets, belts, bows, crossbows, wands, quarterstaves, foci, quivers, and ordinary jewels, not Time-Lost ones) all find their tier and ranges in the tables, by their lines, their required level and the tags of their base. The flasks and the charms weren't checked: the search limit of the trade site was reached.
- **Coverage of the trade ids:** the stats of Exiled Exchange 2 hold every trade id of the modifier lines but 37 of them (checked again on 2026-10-26, an option stat counted once, without its `|option`): 29 explicit ids (the Sacrifice of the Trials of Chaos, a chance of the Expeditions, the Remembrancing and Glorifying lines of the timeless jewels), one fractured id (`Flasks gain # charges per Second`) and 7 rune ids (the levels of some skill gems, `#% increased Spirit for each Corrupted Item Equipped`). The trade site templates find them since 13d, and so would they the modifiers of a patch Exiled Exchange 2 doesn't know yet.
- **The templates of the trade site against the strings of Exiled Exchange 2**, on 2026-10-26: each template of the modifier lines, its `#` written as a number, finds its trade id, from Exiled Exchange 2 for 7,875 English templates and from the trade site for 34; 52 lines with a note (`(Local)`…) aren't found, but the copied text never writes the note. Indexing the templates of both languages takes 6 ms in a release build, when the names of the trade site arrive.

## Bases

The category of a base type (`src/game_data/bases.rs`, step 13h) is looked up by its name in the language of the item, the name its search looks for (the base type of a magic item found among the names of the trade site), without "Exceptional" nor "Superior": `Targe de mammouth` is a `Shield`. It gives the sockets the base usually has, from the table of Exiled Exchange 2 (`getMaxSockets` in `renderer/src/parser/Parser.ts`); a base the data doesn't know has no usual sockets, and its sockets are checked only on a normal or corrupted item. Exiled Exchange 2 lists one base in two categories, `Runemastered Venerable Defender` three times a shield and once a buckler: the generator keeps the category most of its entries have, and prints it.

The category also gives the id of the category on the trade site (step 13j3), searched in place of the base type when the player unchecks it: the table of Exiled Exchange 2 (`CATEGORY_TO_TRADE_ID` in `renderer/src/web/price-check/trade/pathofexile-trade.ts`), and for a flask its tag `life_flask` or `mana_flask`, since the trade site has `flask.life` and `flask.mana` beside `flask` (Exiled Exchange 2 searches `flask`). Checked on the trade site on 2026-10-28: the options of `type_filters.category` in `/api/trade2/data/filters` hold every id of the 30 categories, and a search of `flask.life`, `weapon.warstaff`, `jewel` and `armour.buckler` finds only life flasks, quarterstaves, jewels and bucklers.

Checked on the trade site on 2026-10-27: a base type written with "Exceptional" is refused ("Unknown item base type"), and an offer of an exceptional base has its name in `typeLine` only (`Exceptional Mammoth Targe`, `baseType` `Mammoth Targe`); an exceptional base has more sockets than usual (normal body armours with 3 sockets) or more quality (a normal targe with 24 % and no socket). The filter `equipment_filters.rune_sockets` ("Augmentable Sockets", the one of Exiled Exchange 2) and `total_augment_sockets` ("Total Augment Sockets") find almost the same offers (484 and 488 body armours with 3 sockets or more); `req_filters.lvl` with a maximum and `type_filters.quality` with a minimum find the offers within them.

## Pseudo totals

The totals the trade site adds up from the lines of an item (`pseudo.pseudo_total_life`…) are searched in place of these lines (step 13g). Their rules, in `src/trade/pseudo.rs`, are those of Exiled Exchange 2 (`renderer/src/web/price-check/filters/pseudo/index.ts`), read by the game id of each stat, the same in every language. Checked on the trade site on 2026-10-27, with a search and a fetch each: rare rings without a line of life whose total maximum life is exactly 32 all have `+16 to Strength` (each point counts twice); rare amulets without a line of mana whose total maximum mana is 30 to 40 have 15 to 20 intelligence or `+# to all Attributes`, also from an implicit modifier. The fetched offers don't give the totals. Not checked, taken from Exiled Exchange 2: the runes and enchantments counted like the other groups, and the resistances to an element and chaos counted in the total elemental resistance; if the trade site left out a line the application counts, weighing more than 10 % of the total, the copied item wouldn't pass its own minimum.

## Generator

`tools/game-data/`, a member of the Cargo workspace, writes `assets/game-data/` (step 13b, the modifier tables in 13j1):

- `cargo run -p game-data` reads the latest commit of Exiled Exchange 2 and of the data and the code of the RePoE fork (its code for its license), `cargo run -p game-data -- <commit of Exiled Exchange 2> [<commit of the data of RePoE> [<commit of its code>]]` given ones; the same commits give the same files. `NOTICE.md` names them, with the version of the game RePoE exported.
- `bases-en.ndjson` and `bases-fr.ndjson`: one base per line, its name in the language, its category, for the categories of the equipment, the jewels, the flasks and the charms, and its tags that tell where a modifier rolls (13j1, by its English name); a new category of the game stops the generator until it is decided, like a new group of the trade site. A name of several bases with other tags in RePoE gets no tag, and the generator prints the bases without tags (the Timeless Jewel).
- `stats-en.ndjson` and `stats-fr.ndjson`: one stat per line, so that the changes of a patch read well in a diff, with the fields the application reads (template, game id, better side, strings, trade ids by group, option, inverted); the groups of the Sanctum are left out, and a new group of the trade site stops the generator until it is decided.
- `modifiers.ndjson` (13j1): one table per line, the tiers of a `type` of modifier, prefix or suffix, with the same lines and rolling on the same bases: its lines, each with its template of Exiled Exchange 2 and, for a line of one value that Exiled Exchange 2 can also read written without number, what that value is divided by to be shown when it isn't 1 (none on 2026-10-28; a line of several values with a string without number stops the generator), the weight of each tag (`["crossbow", 1]`, the tags of no released base left out), and each tier with its required level and, for each line, the range of each of its values. Kept: the prefixes and suffixes of the equipment, the jewels, the flasks and the charms (the domains `item`, `misc` and `flask` of RePoE) that roll on a released base (461 tables on 2026-10-28). Left out: those of the essences; the desecrated ones (the domain `desecrated`, see step 13k); those of the Time-Lost jewels, which the game writes within `Small Passive Skills in Radius also grant …` or `Notable Passive Skills in Radius also grant …`, while RePoE gives the bare line (159 tables, step 13k, with a Time-Lost jewel copied by the owner); and those with a line without template of Exiled Exchange 2, which the application can't find. A new handler of the values of a stat stops the generator until it is decided, and so does a stat that rolls but that no value of its line writes.
- `NOTICE.md`: the commits read, the licenses of Exiled Exchange 2 and of the RePoE fork, and the rights of Grinding Gear Games.
- It prints the stats of one language only, and those whose trade ids differ between the languages (a few, 2026-10-26), to look at after a patch: the application reads each language on its own. It prints the modifier lines without a template of Exiled Exchange 2 too, whose modifiers it leaves out (12 on 2026-10-28, the traps and the fishing).

The application reads the file of a language at the first item copied in it (3 ms in a release build), and the modifier tables at the first modifier looked up.

## Decisions

- **Embedded, not downloaded.** The data the application needs is reduced from these sources by a generator kept in the repository, and the result is committed and embedded in the executable (about 1.7 MB before compression for both languages: 2,900 modifiers, 2,500 stats; 330 KB for the 1,500 bases with their tags; and 140 KB for the 461 modifier tables). The application works offline at once, doesn't depend on GitHub at runtime, and a player can't get data that doesn't match their version of the parser. The generator is run again after each patch of the game (Path of Exile 2 1.0 on 2026-12-11), like the parser tests; the update notice of step 15 brings the new data to the players.
- **The trade site templates as a second lookup** (13d), downloaded and cached with the names of the trade site (two more requests a day, the pseudo stats and the Sanctum left out): a line Exiled Exchange 2 doesn't know yet (a new modifier after a patch) still finds its trade id by its template; a line found nowhere isn't a filter, and is written to the log. Until the names arrive at the first launch, only the stats of Exiled Exchange 2 are looked up. A `reduced` line whose template is the `increased` one isn't found there: a new modifier is rarely written both ways, and the next data of Exiled Exchange 2 brings it.
- **The better side** (13c). The `better` value of Exiled Exchange 2 isn't used in the offer tooltip: compared on 2026-10-26 with the words read since step 12b, on every string with `#` of the stats found on equipment, they disagree on 126 English strings, and once each was classified by hand, Exiled Exchange 2 is wrong on most of them (`Lose # Life when you use a Skill`, `#% increased Flask Charges used`, `#% increased effect of Curses on you` better higher). The words were completed from the others (`slower`, `Charges per use`, `against you`, the buffs on the player…; the conversions gray) and kept. A line in French is read with the words of the English string of its stat at the same place (2,616 of the 2,901 French strings line up, with the same `negate` and value), its own words otherwise: it fixed some 640 French strings (`#% de Réduction de l'Armure`). A test counts the strings read higher, lower and unknown in each language, so that a change of the words or of the data shows.
- **Licenses and rules.** Exiled Exchange 2 and the RePoE fork are under the MIT license: their copyright notices go next to the generated data. The data of RePoE and of Exiled Exchange 2 come from the game files and belong to Grinding Gear Games; a free tool using them fits their [developer guidelines](https://www.pathofexile.com/developer/docs), like poe2db and Exiled Exchange 2 itself (see [Images](images.md)). The generated files say where they come from and that they belong to Grinding Gear Games, like `assets/NOTICE.md` for the orbs.
