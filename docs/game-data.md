# Game data

The data of the game the application needs beyond the copied text: the id of each modifier line on the trade site (step 13b, to search it), the category of each base (step 13h, for its usual sockets), and the range of every tier of a modifier (step 13j, for the roll bar and the bounds of the range field). Studied in step 13a, on 2026-10-26, on the copied items of the parser tests.

## Sources compared

| Source | What it gives | Languages | Updated | Rejected or kept |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Trade site, `/api/trade2/data/items` | The base types in each language, grouped by kind (`armour`, `weapon`…), without their category | Every language of the trade site | Always current | Kept for the names of the bases (step 8), not for their category: a group holds the helmets, the body armours and the shields together. |
| Trade site, `/api/trade2/data/stats` | One template per stat and group (`explicit.stat_3299347043`, `# to maximum Life`), one per option for an option stat (`enchant.stat_2954116742\|46224`, `Allocates Arcane Alchemy`), 36 pseudo stats | Every language of the trade site | Always current | **Kept as a second lookup** (13d): a template writes one side of its stat only (`#% reduced Charm Charges used`, without the `increased` one), keeps the `+` sign or not, and ends some with a note the copied text never writes (`(Local)`, `(Global)`, `(Jewel)`, `(Charm)`, `(Flask)`, `(Gold Piles)`, `(Tablets)`). No tier. |
| Exiled Exchange 2, `renderer/public/data/<language>/items.ndjson`, MIT license | Each item by its name in the language: its English name (`refName`), its category (`craftable.category`: `Body Armour`, `Shield`…), its tags | English, French and 8 others | With its stats | **Kept for the category of the bases** (13h). |
| [Exiled Exchange 2](https://github.com/Kvan7/Exiled-Exchange-2), `renderer/public/data/<language>/stats.ndjson`, MIT license | For each stat: its game id (`base_maximum_life`), its English template (`ref`), the strings that match it in the language, with `negate` for the `reduced` variants, its trade ids by group (explicit, implicit, fractured, desecrated, rune, enchant, crafted, pseudo…), and the side that is better (`better`) | English, French and 8 others | At each patch of the game (2026-06, 2026-09) | **Kept for the stat ids.** Generated from the game files by its own tool. No tier. |
| [RePoE fork](https://github.com/repoe-fork/repoe-fork), `https://repoe-fork.github.io/poe2/`: `mods.json`, `base_items.json`, `stat_translations/stat_descriptions.json`; MIT license for the code, the data owned by Grinding Gear Games | Every modifier of the game: its group (`type`), prefix or suffix, its required level, its stats with their ranges, and the tags of the bases where it appears (`spawn_weights`); the tags of each base; the English text of each stat | English only | Within days of a patch (last data 2026-10-19) | **Kept for the tiers.** |
| [poe2db](https://poe2db.tw) | The same tiers, as web pages | Many | Fast | Rejected: no API, its pages would have to be scraped. |

## How they fit together

1. **A copied line → its stat** (`src/game_data/lines.rs`, step 13b). The numbers of the line become `#` (with their sign and the ranges of the advanced copy taken out): `Adds 10(1-16) to 273(239-300) Lightning Damage` → `Adds # to # Lightning Damage`. The template is looked up among the strings of Exiled Exchange 2 in the language of the item, which gives the stat, its English template and its trade ids in each group. The group of the line picks the trade ids: explicit, implicit, fractured, desecrated, crafted, rune, and enchant for the enchantments and the modifiers of the corruption.
   - **The sign** goes into the value (`+15% to Fire Resistance` is `#% to Fire Resistance`), or stays before `#` for the strings that write it (`-#% to all Elemental Resistances per Socket filled`).
   - **The `reduced` variants** are strings of the same stat marked `negate`: `20% reduced Charm Charges used` is -20 of `#% increased Charm Charges used`.
   - **A number written in the string** stays written (`retain 40% of Glory`): the strings with the most `#` are looked up first, then those that keep some numbers. A string without `#` gives its value (`Blind Enemies on Hit` is a chance of 100%), and an option stat its option (the skill of `# to Level of all Arc Skills`).
   - **A stat written on several lines** (`Spells fire # additional Projectiles` / `Spells fire Projectiles in a circle`) is looked up before its lines one by one, within one modifier: in the copy without the advanced descriptions, where each line is a modifier of its own, such a line isn't found.
   - **A local and a global stat** with the same text (`# to Accuracy Rating` of a weapon or of a ring) keep both ids: the copied text doesn't tell them apart, and both are searched in a `count` group of minimum 1 (13e2), which finds the item with either of them.
   - The no-break spaces of French are read as spaces.
   - **The templates of the trade site** (`src/game_data/trade_site.rs`, step 13d) are looked up after the strings of Exiled Exchange 2, the same way, for a line they don't know or have no id for in its group (a modifier added by a patch). A template is looked up without the note at its end, and with it in case a line writes it; the templates written the same are one stat with all their ids (a local and a global stat). Once indexed, the templates leave the names kept in memory (they stay in the cache). The value of such a line is written as its template: 20 for `20% reduced Charm Charges used` and `#% reduced Charm Charges used`. In another language than English, its words are those of the English template of its id, for the better side of the tooltip (13c).
   - A line found nowhere, without an id in its group, or found on the trade site only, is written to the log.
2. **A stat → its tiers.** The English template leads to the stats of RePoE with the same text: one template can be several game stats, a global one and a local one (`base_maximum_energy_shield` and `local_energy_shield`, `base_spirit` and `base_spirit_from_equipment`), and the one a modifier uses on this base is the one found among its modifiers. The base type of the item gives its tags (`Gemini Crossbow` → `crossbow`, `ranged`, `two_hand_weapon`…); a French base type gets its English name from the items of Exiled Exchange 2 (`refName`). The modifiers of the base for a stat are those, prefix or suffix as the copy says, whose first weight with a tag of the base is above zero; grouped by `type` and sorted by required level, the last one is tier 1.
3. **Several groups for one stat**, e.g. `#% increased Physical Damage` alone or with Accuracy Rating: the modifier with the same number of lines as the copied one, and whose tier range holds the copied ranges.

## Checks on the copied items

- **Stat ids:** every modifier line of the English and French items found its stat, except the lines of the "fixed value" marker of the game, which the parser already takes out.
- **Tiers:** the tier written by the advanced copy and its range, 23 lines of 5 rare and magic items (a crossbow, a bow, a mace, a staff and a body armour in French), all found again from RePoE: `Electrocuting` T2 `1–16 to 239–300` among 10 tiers, `Champion's` T4 `45–54 · 98–123` (the hybrid with Accuracy), the energy shield T4 `61–73` among 11, the spirit T5 `43–46` among 8, the life regeneration T3 `1386–1740` per minute shown as `23.1–29` per second. The values of some stats are stored scaled: per minute, per ten thousand (`7–7.9%` leech is `700–790`); the advanced copy gives the scale to check them against.
- **Coverage of the trade ids:** the stats of Exiled Exchange 2 hold every trade id of the modifier lines but 37 of them (checked again on 2026-10-26, an option stat counted once, without its `|option`): 29 explicit ids (the Sacrifice of the Trials of Chaos, a chance of the Expeditions, the Remembrancing and Glorifying lines of the timeless jewels), one fractured id (`Flasks gain # charges per Second`) and 7 rune ids (the levels of some skill gems, `#% increased Spirit for each Corrupted Item Equipped`). The trade site templates find them since 13d, and so would they the modifiers of a patch Exiled Exchange 2 doesn't know yet.
- **The templates of the trade site against the strings of Exiled Exchange 2**, on 2026-10-26: each template of the modifier lines, its `#` written as a number, finds its trade id, from Exiled Exchange 2 for 7,875 English templates and from the trade site for 34; 52 lines with a note (`(Local)`…) aren't found, but the copied text never writes the note. Indexing the templates of both languages takes 6 ms in a release build, when the names of the trade site arrive.

## Bases

The category of a base type (`src/game_data/bases.rs`, step 13h) is looked up by its name in the language of the item, the name its search looks for (the base type of a magic item found among the names of the trade site), without "Exceptional" nor "Superior": `Targe de mammouth` is a `Shield`. It gives the sockets the base usually has, from the table of Exiled Exchange 2 (`getMaxSockets` in `renderer/src/parser/Parser.ts`); a base the data doesn't know has no usual sockets, and its sockets are checked only on a normal or corrupted item. Exiled Exchange 2 lists one base in two categories, `Runemastered Venerable Defender` three times a shield and once a buckler: the generator keeps the category most of its entries have, and prints it.

Checked on the trade site on 2026-10-27: a base type written with "Exceptional" is refused ("Unknown item base type"), and an offer of an exceptional base has its name in `typeLine` only (`Exceptional Mammoth Targe`, `baseType` `Mammoth Targe`); an exceptional base has more sockets than usual (normal body armours with 3 sockets) or more quality (a normal targe with 24 % and no socket). The filter `equipment_filters.rune_sockets` ("Augmentable Sockets", the one of Exiled Exchange 2) and `total_augment_sockets` ("Total Augment Sockets") find almost the same offers (484 and 488 body armours with 3 sockets or more); `req_filters.lvl` with a maximum and `type_filters.quality` with a minimum find the offers within them.

## Pseudo totals

The totals the trade site adds up from the lines of an item (`pseudo.pseudo_total_life`…) are searched in place of these lines (step 13g). Their rules, in `src/trade/pseudo.rs`, are those of Exiled Exchange 2 (`renderer/src/web/price-check/filters/pseudo/index.ts`), read by the game id of each stat, the same in every language. Checked on the trade site on 2026-10-27, with a search and a fetch each: rare rings without a line of life whose total maximum life is exactly 32 all have `+16 to Strength` (each point counts twice); rare amulets without a line of mana whose total maximum mana is 30 to 40 have 15 to 20 intelligence or `+# to all Attributes`, also from an implicit modifier. The fetched offers don't give the totals. Not checked, taken from Exiled Exchange 2: the runes and enchantments counted like the other groups, and the resistances to an element and chaos counted in the total elemental resistance; if the trade site left out a line the application counts, weighing more than 10 % of the total, the copied item wouldn't pass its own minimum.

## Generator

`tools/game-data/`, a member of the Cargo workspace, writes `assets/game-data/` (step 13b):

- `cargo run -p game-data` reads the latest commit of Exiled Exchange 2, `cargo run -p game-data -- <commit>` a given one; the same commit gives the same files.
- `bases-en.ndjson` and `bases-fr.ndjson`: one base per line, its name in the language and its category, for the categories of the equipment, the jewels, the flasks and the charms; a new category of the game stops the generator until it is decided, like a new group of the trade site.
- `stats-en.ndjson` and `stats-fr.ndjson`: one stat per line, so that the changes of a patch read well in a diff, with the fields the application reads (template, game id, better side, strings, trade ids by group, option, inverted); the groups of the Sanctum are left out, and a new group of the trade site stops the generator until it is decided.
- `NOTICE.md`: the commit read, the license of Exiled Exchange 2, and the rights of Grinding Gear Games.
- It prints the stats of one language only, and those whose trade ids differ between the languages (a few, 2026-10-26), to look at after a patch: the application reads each language on its own.

The application reads the file of a language at the first item copied in it (3 ms in a release build). The modifiers and bases of RePoE come into the generator with the modifier tables (13j).

## Decisions

- **Embedded, not downloaded.** The data the application needs is reduced from these sources by a generator kept in the repository, and the result is committed and embedded in the executable (about 1.7 MB before compression for both languages: 2,900 modifiers, 2,500 stats; and 170 KB for the 1,500 bases). The application works offline at once, doesn't depend on GitHub at runtime, and a player can't get data that doesn't match their version of the parser. The generator is run again after each patch of the game (Path of Exile 2 1.0 on 2026-12-11), like the parser tests; the update notice of step 15 brings the new data to the players.
- **The trade site templates as a second lookup** (13d), downloaded and cached with the names of the trade site (two more requests a day, the pseudo stats and the Sanctum left out): a line Exiled Exchange 2 doesn't know yet (a new modifier after a patch) still finds its trade id by its template; a line found nowhere isn't a filter, and is written to the log. Until the names arrive at the first launch, only the stats of Exiled Exchange 2 are looked up. A `reduced` line whose template is the `increased` one isn't found there: a new modifier is rarely written both ways, and the next data of Exiled Exchange 2 brings it.
- **The better side** (13c). The `better` value of Exiled Exchange 2 isn't used in the offer tooltip: compared on 2026-10-26 with the words read since step 12b, on every string with `#` of the stats found on equipment, they disagree on 126 English strings, and once each was classified by hand, Exiled Exchange 2 is wrong on most of them (`Lose # Life when you use a Skill`, `#% increased Flask Charges used`, `#% increased effect of Curses on you` better higher). The words were completed from the others (`slower`, `Charges per use`, `against you`, the buffs on the player…; the conversions gray) and kept. A line in French is read with the words of the English string of its stat at the same place (2,616 of the 2,901 French strings line up, with the same `negate` and value), its own words otherwise: it fixed some 640 French strings (`#% de Réduction de l'Armure`). A test counts the strings read higher, lower and unknown in each language, so that a change of the words or of the data shows.
- **Licenses and rules.** Exiled Exchange 2 is under the MIT license: its copyright notice goes next to the generated data. The data of RePoE and of Exiled Exchange 2 come from the game files and belong to Grinding Gear Games; a free tool using them fits their [developer guidelines](https://www.pathofexile.com/developer/docs), like poe2db and Exiled Exchange 2 itself (see [Images](images.md)). The generated files say where they come from and that they belong to Grinding Gear Games, like `assets/NOTICE.md` for the orbs.
