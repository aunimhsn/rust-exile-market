The first version of Exile Market, a price checker for Path of Exile 2, free and open source, for Windows and Linux. It is a pre-alpha, out early to hear from the players: expect bugs, and report them through the bug icon of the title bar.

The download page: https://exile-market.sylent.tools

## Download

| System | Archive |
|---|---|
| Windows | `exile-market-windows-x64.zip` |
| Linux | `exile-market-linux-x64.tar.gz` |

Unpack it and start `exile-market.exe` (Windows) or `exile-market` (Linux). There is nothing to install. The executable isn't signed for now, so Windows may show an alert at the first launch: "More info", then "Run anyway". The checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

The macOS archives (`exile-market-macos-arm64.tar.gz`, `exile-market-macos-x64.tar.gz`) are for players who run the Windows client through a compatibility layer: there, only Ctrl+C copies an item, without the window over the game. They are less tested.

## Before you play

Put the game in **windowed fullscreen** or in a **window**. Exclusive fullscreen isn't supported: Ctrl+D copies nothing, and the D reaches the game.

## What it does

- **Ctrl+D**, with the pointer over an item in the game: its price in a small window over the game. **Ctrl+C**, the copy of the game: the price in the main window.
- **Currencies and uniques**: their price at once, in three currencies, from poe2scout.
- **Rare, magic and normal items, and jewels**: their stats and tiers, each stat a filter to tick, then the offers of similar items on the official trade site, and a price estimated from them. A search only runs when you click it.
- The game in English or French, and the interface in both.

## Known limits

- On Linux, the hotkey and the window over the game were tried on KDE Plasma alone. On another desktop (GNOME…), they may not behave the same: a report helps.
- Windows is less tested than Linux.
