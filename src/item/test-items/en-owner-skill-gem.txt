Item Class: Skill Gems
Rarity: Gem
Flame Wall
--------
Spell, AoE, Fire, Duration, Repeatable
Level: 2
Cost: 12 Mana
Cast Time: 1.00s
Critical Hit Chance: 5.00%
--------
Requires: Level 3, 9 Int
--------
Sockets: G G 
--------
Create a wall of Fire in front of you, which Ignites everything within its area. Any Projectiles fired through the wall by you and Allies deal added Fire damage. Consumes a Lightning Infusion if possible to also add Lightning damage to the Projectiles.
--------
Wall
--------
Wall deals 2.3 Fire Damage per second
Wall duration is 4.6 seconds
Wall length is 5.1 metres
Ignites as though dealing 7 to 11 Fire damage
--------
Projectile Damage
--------
Projectiles which pass through the wall deal 2 to 4 Added Fire Damage
Projectiles which pass through the Infused wall deal 1 to 6 Added Lightning Damage
Wall length is 5.1 metres
--------
Skills can be managed in the Skills Panel.
