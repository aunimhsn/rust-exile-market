# Game data notice

Generated by `tools/game-data` (`cargo run -p game-data`, see `docs/game-data.md`): don't edit these files by hand, generate them again.

`stats-en.ndjson` and `stats-fr.ndjson` are the stats of [Exiled Exchange 2](https://github.com/Kvan7/Exiled-Exchange-2) (`renderer/public/data/<language>/stats.ndjson`), commit `cca30662bf31eaf38bd711e2ec1a6b899a06c40e` of 2026-09-06T20:51:34Z, reduced to the fields the application reads and without the groups of the Sanctum. `bases-en.ndjson` and `bases-fr.ndjson` are the bases of its items (`renderer/public/data/<language>/items.ndjson`), of the same commit: the name and the category of each base of equipment, jewel, flask, charm, waystone or tablet, with the tags of the base from RePoE.

`modifiers.ndjson` holds the tiers of the prefixes and suffixes of the equipment, the jewels, the flasks, the charms, the waystones and the tablets, from the data of the [RePoE fork](https://github.com/repoe-fork/poe2) (`data/mods.json`, `data/base_items.json`, `data/stat_translations/stat_descriptions.json` and `data/stat_translations/tablet_stat_descriptions.json`), commit `75885a099f2e9be1619aa3c793fe6ab0754a511f` of 2026-10-19T04:56:41Z, export of the version 0.5.5.4.2 of the game, their lines written as the templates of Exiled Exchange 2. The bases of the modifiers only an essence adds, which RePoE doesn't give, are those of [Craft of Exile](https://www.craftofexile.com), checked on the trade site.
